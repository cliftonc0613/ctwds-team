# Blog Skills Reference

30 sub-skills from the `/blog` content engine — dual-optimized for Google rankings and AI citations.

---

## 1. Content Creation
- `write <topic>` — Write a new blog post from scratch (research → outline → draft → SEO → score)
- `rewrite <file>` — Optimize an existing post with AI detection and anti-AI patterns
- `outline <topic>` — SERP-informed content outline with competitive gap analysis
- `brief <topic>` — Detailed content brief with template recommendation and distribution plan

## 2. Quality & Analysis
- `analyze <file>` — 5-category 100-point quality audit with AI content detection
- `factcheck <file>` — Verify statistics against cited sources
- `seo-check <file>` — Post-writing SEO validation (title, meta, headings, links, OG)
- `geo <file>` — AI citation readiness audit with 0-100 GEO score

## 3. Site-Wide Audit & Planning
- `audit [dir]` — Full-site blog health assessment with parallel subagents
- `cannibalization [dir]` — Detect keyword cannibalization across posts with severity scoring
- `calendar [monthly|quarterly]` — Editorial calendar with decay detection and 60/30/10 content mix
- `strategy <niche>` — Positioning, topic clusters, and AI citation surface strategy

## 4. Technical & Schema
- `schema <file>` — Generate JSON-LD schema markup (BlogPosting, Person, FAQ, Breadcrumb)
- `taxonomy [suggest|sync|audit]` — Tag/category management across WordPress, Shopify, Ghost, Strapi, Sanity

## 5. Research & Intelligence
- `discourse <topic>` — Research what people are saying in the last 30 days (API-free)
- `cluster [plan|execute] <seed>` — Semantic topic-cluster planning + hub-and-spoke execution
- `notebooklm <question>` — Query NotebookLM for source-grounded research from your documents

## 6. Brand & Voice
- `brand [init|show|update]` — Generate BRAND.md + VOICE.md context files auto-loaded by all sub-skills
- `persona [create|list|use|show]` — Manage writing personas and voice profiles

## 7. Repurposing & Distribution
- `repurpose <file>` — Cross-platform repurposing (social, email, YouTube, Reddit)
- `flow [find|optimize|win|prompts|sync]` — FLOW framework prompts (evidence-led, 30 blog-applicable)

## 8. Multilingual
- `multilingual <topic> --languages <codes>` — Write + translate + localize + emit hreflang in one command
- `translate <file> --to <codes>` — SEO-optimized translation with format preservation
- `localize <file> --locale <code>` — Cultural deep-adaptation (DACH, FR, ES, JA, custom)
- `locale-audit [dir]` — Multilingual content QA (completeness, hreflang, parity, freshness)

## 9. Media & Assets
- `image [generate|edit|setup]` — AI image generation and editing via Gemini
- `audio [generate|voices|setup]` — Generate audio narration (summary, full, or dialogue modes; 30 voices)
- `chart` — Generate inline SVG data visualizations (internal — auto-invoked during write/rewrite)

## 10. Google Data
- `google [pagespeed|crux|gsc|ga4|nlp|youtube|keywords]` — Live Google API data: PSI, CrUX CWV, Search Console, Analytics, NLP, YouTube, Keywords
