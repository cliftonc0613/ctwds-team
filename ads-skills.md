# Ads Skills Reference

18 skills from the `/ads` paid advertising engine — multi-platform auditing, creative production, and campaign strategy.

---

## 1. Full Audit
- `audit` — Full multi-platform audit: 250+ checks across all platforms simultaneously, per-platform health scores, prioritized action plan

## 2. Platform Audits
- `google` — Google Ads deep analysis: Search, PMax, AI Max, Display, Demand Gen (80 checks)
- `meta` — Meta Ads deep analysis: Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Advantage+ (50 checks)
- `youtube` — YouTube Ads analysis: Skippable, Shorts, Demand Gen, CTV formats
- `linkedin` — LinkedIn Ads deep analysis: B2B, Lead Gen, Thought Leader, ABM (27 checks)
- `tiktok` — TikTok Ads deep analysis: Creative, Shop, Smart+, post-USDS (28 checks)
- `microsoft` — Microsoft/Bing Ads deep analysis: Copilot, Bing, Google import validation (24 checks)
- `apple` — Apple Search Ads: CPPs, AdAttributionKit, TAP coverage (35+ checks)
- `amazon` — Amazon Ads: Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, ACOS (30+ checks)

## 3. Creative Production
- `creative` — Cross-platform creative quality audit: copy, video, image, format diversity, fatigue detection
- `generate` — AI image generation for ad creatives from campaign brief and brand profile (Gemini)
- `photoshoot` — AI product photography in 5 styles: Studio, Floating, Ingredient, In Use, Lifestyle (1:1 and 9:16)

## 4. Strategy & Intelligence
- `plan <type>` — Strategic ad plan with industry-specific templates: platform selection, architecture, budget, creative roadmap
- `competitor` — Competitor ad intelligence: copy analysis, creative strategy, estimated spend, gaps across Google/Meta/LinkedIn/TikTok/Microsoft
- `dna <url>` — Brand DNA extractor: scans a URL and outputs `brand-profile.json` (colors, typography, tone, imagery style)
- `create` — Campaign concept and copy brief generator: reads `brand-profile.json` and outputs `campaign-brief.md`

## 5. Performance Optimization
- `landing` — Landing page quality assessment: message match, page speed, mobile, trust signals, form optimization, conversion potential
- `budget` — Budget allocation and bidding strategy review: 70/20/10 rule, 3x Kill Rule, 20% scaling rule, kill list and scale list
